Power law

Used this week

The long-run curve through bitcoin’s daily price since 2010. It shows where price usually sits. It is not a promise of a future price.

Today’s price can move the gap to the power law. The curve itself is redrawn only on Friday, using prices known that day. Being 55% under is why this week says Add. It does not make the answer surer.

Z-score

Used as a pair

Bitcoin’s price divided by gold’s price, compared with the past year of Fridays. A low z-score means bitcoin is unusually cheap next to gold. This is not the on-chain MVRV z-score.

From 12 Sep 2025 to the setup on 21 Nov 2025, bitcoin fell about 27% and gold rose about 11%. About a quarter of that cheapening was gold rising, which is why this z-score is marked “gold rose.” The answer stays the same. We show the split so a gold rally is not mistaken for bitcoin getting cheap on its own.

Realized price

Sets the pace

The average price paid for the bitcoin that exists, weighted by when it last moved. Under realized price, one larger sum is sliced across Fridays.

Today bitcoin is 1.22 times realized price, so the extra weekly slice stays off. While price was under realized price, about 79% of those weeks were up at least 50% a year later. That happened in four separate stretches, not in 79 separate bets. The exact weekly pace was not tested.

Sell roll

Quiet

The power law and the z-score on the expensive side. It can pause new buying for up to a year. It does not sell the bitcoin you hold.

In 6 of 8 finished cases the next year was flat or down. Two cases were followed by very large gains, which is why this never sells your coins. It also missed the 2019–20 drop and the 2025–26 drop. A cautious reading is about 6 times in 10.

    One named calculation, such as the power law or the z-score. An indicator is not itself the instruction.
    Today’s readings
    The live price and the current value of each indicator the site watches. They can change when you open the page. They do not change the answer until Friday.
    This week’s answer
    The one instruction set each Friday. Pause, Go slow, Steady, Add, or Buy strongly. The five marks show where it sits. They are not a second list of indicators.
    Checklist
    The conditions in the order Friday checks them. The first one that fits sets the answer. Below the average price paid is a pace on that list, not a sixth answer.
    Already happened
    The buy-cross week is over. It was 18 Sep, plus one extra Friday on 25 Sep.
    Does not fit
    Friday checked the condition and it was false.
    Not used
    A later condition. An earlier one already won, so the walk stopped.
    Cautious reading
    A smaller number than the raw win rate, so four successes in four tries are not treated as a sure thing. About 6 or 7 times in 10, depending on the rule.
    Stretch
    One continuous run of similar weeks. Overlapping weeks are not counted as separate tries.
    Open
    The year after the event is not finished, so that event is shown and not added to the record.
