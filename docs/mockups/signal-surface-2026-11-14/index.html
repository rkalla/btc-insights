Three ways to show the call

Interactive mockups. Readings are the 13 Nov 2026 Friday stamp and the 14 Nov 2026 live print. Charts are schematics.

  • A · The desk — the call beside the instruments that built it. Models.
  • B · Two clocks — a live tape over the Friday stamp. Library.
  • C · Atlas — one time axis, one lane per input. Power-law lane.
  • D · Composite — today’s numbers, Friday’s answer, Desk-style indicators. Long-run trend.

These pages are proposals. They are not the live site.
