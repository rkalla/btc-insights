# GOAL

Develop a dashboard that a Bitcoin holder can open on any day, on a spectrum from "Exit" to "All in," and that shows how much history stands behind each call. The levels on the page are the latest print. The official action is the one set at the Friday close, because that weekly sample is the clock the record was built on. Friday is not a finding that one weekday is a better day to act.

Every action shows its record, a confidence floor, and the number of independent episodes behind it. The floor is the lower end of a Wilson 90% interval, the method that matches the floors already published for the buy cross, the sell roll, and the lift. High confidence means a floor of 80% or more. On that method, eight wins in eight tries reach about 75%, not 80%. Eleven wins in eleven tries, or 19 of 20, are what clear 80%. An action may appear before it earns that badge. It may never wear a confidence it has not earned.

Actions are judged against the default of steady buying and holding. The scores that matter are coins accumulated, and after-tax dollars once the account type is known. Timing signals are judged at 1 year. The 5-year and 10-year views are about allocation and whether the long-term thesis still holds. A long hold is a base rate, not a timing signal.

Signals are scored on all complete data. Prices start in 2010. Finished 12-month scoring starts in 2012. An open episode is shown as open. It is not called a finished test.

The dashboard answers two questions.

What to do with cash runs from a pause, through the standing contribution and a spread of new money, to an immediate buy. The boldest buy is about $100,000 of cash already on hand, spent when the buy cross fires. That sum is not a reserve refilled during the years between crosses.

What to do with coins already held is hold, trim in steps down to an allocation ceiling the holder has set, or exit. Exit is reserved for a break in the long-term thesis. A cycle high is not that break.

The purpose is to accumulate more Bitcoin than steady buying and holding, over 1, 5, and 10 year horizons.
